### Brand rules

1. On a BT SHARE product page, give the **product** primary visual prominence; identify **ZAEROX** as the technology brand and credit **X10 THINK** as founder.
2. Preserve supplied artwork proportions; do not stretch or recolor logos without approval.
3. Transparent white wordmarks require a dark contrasting surface. Use the black-background ZAEROX variation on mixed/light GitHub themes.
4. Keep the tagline exactly **Innovation Beyond Limits** under ZAEROX, not an invented BT SHARE slogan.
5. Avoid unsupported launch dates, compatibility claims, hardware certifications or software release badges.
6. Before shipping an app, website or physical product, confirm artwork permission, trademark usage and appropriate legal notices with the company.

## Product description for public-facing drafts

> **BT SHARE** is an emerging Bluetooth audio-sharing initiative from **ZAEROX**, founded by **X10 THINK**. Its long-term goal is affordable shared listening through compatible wireless headphones and earbuds. The initial college prototype targets two Bluetooth audio receivers; a broader commercial design is being researched and has not yet been independently validated.

This is a brand description, **not** a completed-hardware or product-for-sale claim.
